Water complaints should include location details, landmark, and issue duration.
Road complaints should include pothole size, exact street name, and safety risk level.
Garbage complaints should include pickup delay days and nearest collection point.
Electricity complaints should include outage duration, area code, and transformer details.
All complaints should be acknowledged with a ticket reference and expected resolution timeline.
Escalation to district officer is required if complaint remains unresolved for more than 7 days.
Citizen communication must remain respectful, concise, and action oriented.
